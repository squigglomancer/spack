How to play

Click a cell to cycle blank → X → O. Every board can have at most 5 of either symbol, and across the whole super-board neither X nor O can win more than 5 of the 9 boards. A board tinted pink/blue with a corner label has a given winner (X/O); a grey tint with "–" means a given draw; no tint or label means the winner is unknown.

The pink and blue numbers along the edges give the required X and O counts for that row or column. The Hint button won't fill anything in for you — it briefly highlights the row, column, or board where a deduction is currently possible, so you know where to look.

The message above the board reveals who wins the super-board overall — X, O, or a cat's game (nobody) — even though most of the individual board winners feeding into it stay hidden.

Undo steps back through your moves one at a time, all the way to the start of the puzzle; Redo steps forward again.

Daily puzzles are the same for everyone each day and your progress is saved automatically in this browser. Free Play puzzles are random and not saved.
